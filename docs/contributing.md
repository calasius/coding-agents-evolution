# Contributing

This project is a source-backed reference document. Contributions should improve accuracy, structure, source quality, or clarity.

## Source Standards

Prefer:

- Academic papers, especially arXiv pages or conference proceedings.
- Official company engineering blogs and product announcements.
- Primary benchmark pages and leaderboards.
- Direct posts from named practitioners when the claim is about their own work.

Avoid as the only source:

- Unsourced social-media posts.
- Wikipedia.
- Funding rumors.
- Reposted summaries.
- Vendor marketing pages that do not link to underlying evidence.

## Claim Standards

Every factual claim involving dates, benchmark scores, funding, product launches, paper publication, or company adoption should have a citation.

When a claim is interpretive rather than factual, make the uncertainty explicit. Use wording such as "arguably," "the practical effect was," "the document frames this as," or "the evidence suggests."

## Editorial Tone

Keep the document direct and skeptical. Distinguish between:

- Established facts.
- Practitioner interpretations.
- Academic findings.
- Market claims.
- Speculative frontier work.

The goal is not to promote a tool, company, or framework. The goal is to explain how the field evolved and which ideas survived contact with practice.

## Suggested Contribution Types

- Replace weak references with primary sources.
- Add missing papers or product launches with dates.
- Correct benchmark numbers or clarify benchmark variants.
- Tighten overconfident claims.
- Improve diagrams when they clarify the argument.
- Move duplicated material into a single canonical section.
